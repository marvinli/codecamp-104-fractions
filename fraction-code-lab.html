The Halves Machine

Computers are amazing at math, but they have one strange habit. Ask it a question and look closely at the answer.

show(…) prints an answer

Why does this happen?

A computer stores every number by stacking halves: 1 over 2, 1 over 4, 1 over 8, 1 over 16, and so on. That's the only kind of piece it has.

Some fractions can be built exactly from halves. Some can't, no matter how many pieces you use. 1 over 10 is one of those. The computer uses about 50 pieces, gets super close, and stops. Scientists call this floating point.

You, writing 1/30.3333333…Our tens-based decimals can't finish thirds.
A computer, storing 1/100.1000000000000000055…Its halves-based numbers can't finish tenths.
